# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Global Media Discovery welcomes respectful, constructive participation from
people of every background and level of experience.

Examples of positive participation include:

- discussing technical decisions with evidence and empathy;
- respecting differing experience, language, identity, and viewpoints;
- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- accepting responsibility, correcting mistakes, and helping others learn;
- protecting credentials, private data, and the safety of project users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, publishing
private information, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
release comments, and other spaces where someone represents this project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that do not
meet this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where
possible, and focused on protecting the community.

Report repository abuse through GitHub's reporting tools or contact the
[repository owner](https://github.com/unknown0152). Do not include credentials
or sensitive personal information in a public issue. Suspected security
vulnerabilities must use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

No one who is directly involved in a complaint should make the final
enforcement decision alone when another maintainer is available.
